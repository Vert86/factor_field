How To Play

🎯 Objective

Reduce all numbers on the grid to 1 by selecting prime numbers that divide them.

🎮 Game Rules

  1. Select a Prime: Click any prime number button below the grid
  2. Division Happens: ALL tiles divisible by that prime are divided simultaneously
  3. Move Limit: You can only make as many moves as the optimal solution
  4. Repeat: Keep selecting primes until all tiles show 1
  5. Win: When every tile equals 1 within the move limit, you complete the level!

💡 Pro Tips

  • No Visual Feedback: Prime buttons never gray out - clicking a used-up prime = instant failure!
  • Track Mentally: You must remember which primes you've used and how many times
  • Move Limit: You can only make as many moves as the optimal solution - no room for error!
  • Calculate first: Work out the entire sequence mentally BEFORE clicking any primes
  • Look for patterns: Numbers like 8 (2³) need prime 2 applied three times
  • Large primes: Primes like 7, 11, 13 usually only appear once in numbers
